Flooring: accepted quote to completed fitting

This release implements the first connected flooring phase within the existing CRM. It retains the existing customer, sales-order, invoice, purchasing and stock ledgers. It does not represent completion of the full 69-page blueprint.

How to use it

  1. Open a flooring lead, record room measurements and catalogue product lines, then move it to Quote.
  2. Select Accept quote and record the customer's agreement. A manager must review quotes below the configured margin threshold.
  3. Open Materials & fitting. A manager/team lead selects Allocate & prepare purchases, reviews and sends any draft POs from Purchasing, then records receipts and allocates received stock.
  4. Confirm the fitting date, time window and fitter with the customer. Accounts issues the linked invoice and records payment through the existing ledger.
  5. The assigned fitter starts the job when materials and payment are ready, uploads completion photos, and records customer sign-off. Report Issue creates a linked service case.

Explicit limits and next phases

The existing 69-page blueprint remains the wider target. This phase does not include irregular-room overrides, carpet roll/cut optimization, pack-to-area conversion, quote revision/cancellation after acceptance, partial-fitting completion, a drag-and-drop fitting calendar, separate surveyor roles, offline measure drafts or automated quote emails. Fitting currently uses the existing customer's site address. Underlay/accessory extras are charges only; stock requirements must be entered as product lines. Supplier POs are drafts requiring review and sending; nothing is emailed automatically. Completion photos plus recorded sign-off are supported, not a drawn-signature capture pad.

Profit snapshots use the configured standard VAT assumption; Accounts must review tax treatment before invoice issuance. Actual costs remain reviewed through the order cost sheet. Unattended reminders still need the existing jobs:run scheduler on production deployment.

Broader pending blueprint work includes production Shopify event/retry and two-way sync, configurable approval execution, wider server pagination, full returns/replacement reconciliation and the remaining module acceptance criteria.
