Claims, replacements, returns and refunds

Open Customer service and select a case to use the connected after-sales panel. Each order also has an After-sales tab with its case history. This extends the existing CRM and ledger; no additional application or sales-order copy is created.

Blueprint §13 implementation

AreaWorking behaviour
Claim and evidenceCases remain linked to the existing customer/order/product/supplier. Select an affected product from the original order and a quantity within the original sale. Existing photo/document attachments and team activity remain available. Cases without an explicit owner default to their creator.
Customer and supplier updatesSeparate dated communication logs, last customer update and next customer/supplier commitments. Calls and emails are recorded after staff perform them; the interface does not send messages. Queue filters include customer update due, supplier chase due and replacement overdue. The queue's due date uses the earliest commitment. The existing jobs runner creates deduplicated internal notifications and stops generating them for resolved/closed cases.
Replacement supplyManagers/team leads allocate available stock and draft a supplier purchase for any shortage. Staff enter the agreed supplier unit cost, including zero only for an agreed free replacement. The case owns the replacement PO link, so replacement supply does not count toward original sales demand. Review/confirm the purchase, receive goods through Inventory and prepare again to allocate arrivals. Repeated preparation does not duplicate its purchase.
Replacement deliveryFully allocated replacement units create a real delivery job. The existing assignment, date/window, customer confirmation and proof controls apply. Case allocations are isolated from the original order groups. Replacement dispatch consumes only its reserved stock and does not mark the original sale delivered again. Generic inventory dispatch cannot bypass case delivery proof.
ReturnsReceive whole units against delivered stock reservations on the original order. Partial returns preserve the unreturned quantity. Restock adds saleable stock; Write off records Return and Damage movements atomically with no increase in saleable stock. Quantities cannot exceed delivered units or the case requirement. The balance lookup now uses the unique product/location pair, including balances created with nonstandard IDs.
Refunds and goodwillA case can request an exact invoice-linked management approval. A different manager must approve it. Accounts/Management uses the existing invoice refund workflow and links the actual recorded refund to the case. A refund cannot be credited to two cases. Approval alone is not payment evidence. Rejected requests may be revised; customer-agreed resolution without a refund must use the no-further-action outcome.
Resolution / reopeningResolution records an outcome, explanation and explicit customer confirmation. Active replacement delivery, outstanding purchase, required return or requested refund evidence blocks resolution. Combined remedy requires two recorded outcomes. Staff may reopen a resolved/closed case with an audited reason; prior evidence remains.
Stopping a replacementA manager/team lead can stop an undispatched replacement with a reason. Unsent purchase drafts and undispatched jobs are marked cancelled; held stock is released. Sent supplier purchases must first have cancellation confirmed and recorded. Dispatched goods cannot be undone by cancellation and require a return/further case.

Team workflow

  1. Create a case from the order, set type/priority/owner and attach evidence.
  2. Log the customer commitment and supplier response with separate follow-up dates.
  3. Select the affected order item; prepare replacement stock/purchasing when needed.
  4. Receive supplier goods, allocate and create the replacement delivery. The delivery team records customer proof.
  5. Inspect physical returns and choose Restock or Write off.
  6. For monetary remedies, request approval, record the invoice refund and link the payment evidence.
  7. Resolve with a structured outcome and customer confirmation, then close administratively. Reopen if further investigation is needed.

Boundaries

  • One affected catalogue product/quantity, replacement purchase, replacement delivery and linked refund per case. Use separate cases for different items or a new replacement cycle after cancellation. Partial returns are supported; partial replacement deliveries within one case are not.
  • Whole furniture items/packs are supported. Bespoke spare parts without a catalogue product, measured flooring material returns, replacement fitting/assembly scheduling, supplier credit-note accounting and stock cost-layer valuation need further work.
  • Damaged returns may be written off explicitly. Quarantine/inspection queues and repair-stock locations are not introduced in this phase.
  • External supplier/customer emails, carrier cancellation, physical payment transfer and scheduled jobs deployment require the existing operational arrangements. The CRM records those actions; it does not execute an external refund or send an email by itself.
  • Additional service/remedy costs should be reviewed through the existing expenses/actual-cost workflow. This phase does not automatically reconcile supplier credits or replacement charges into final profitability.
  • Historical simple cases retain their legacy status path. Cases with tracked remedies must use the guarded resolution action.
  • This completes this connected after-sales phase, not every item in the complete CRM blueprint. Production Shopify credentials/sync rollout and team pilot remain separate.
