Furniture orders → stock → delivery → assembly

Open an existing furniture order and select Fulfilment, or use Manage fulfilment in its header. This extends the existing CRM, shared PostgreSQL records and permission model. No new platform or duplicate ledger was introduced.

Implemented in this phase

Blueprint areaWorking behaviour
§8 Inventory / §9 Sales ordersThe order workspace shows ordered, allocated, delivered, incoming and uncovered quantities per product and shipment. Manager/team-lead preparation reserves available physical stock and creates supplier purchase drafts for the uncovered balance. Existing incoming quantities count only once across shipment groups. Repeated/concurrent preparation does not duplicate demand.
§6 Purchasing / §7 Supplier trackingDraft purchases use catalogue suppliers, SKUs and current supplier costs, linked to the existing order/customer. Open each purchase to review and follow the existing confirmation, supplier-tracking and goods-receipt workflow. Rerun allocation after receipt. Draft creation does not send an external purchase order.
§9 Partial fulfilmentSplit whole-unit furniture quantities into separate shipments before delivery/assembly jobs exist. The original shipment retains allocations first; any excess allocation moves to the new shipment. Warehouse balances, sales quantities and invoice totals stay unchanged. Staff record a reason/customer arrangement. Stale requests are rejected and the split is audited. Existing message packs are invalidated for review.
§10 Delivery / §11 AssemblyCreate a linked delivery job from a fully allocated shipment. Assembly routes create a linked assembly job. Existing scheduling, assignment, customer confirmation, proof and sign-off controls remain authoritative. Each shipment dispatches only its own reserved stock. Completing one shipment does not complete others.
§17 ProfitabilityThe order workflow links to the existing actual-cost sheet and shows whether costs have been reviewed. Profit must remain provisional until costs are reviewed and product costs verified. This phase does not add stock cost-layer accounting or supplier-invoice matching.
§29 Integrity / §32 Release gateManual receipt flags no longer bypass delivery stock checks. Generic order evidence cannot override connected fulfilment. Product/route revision is blocked once active supply/stock/jobs exist. Job shipment links cannot be reassigned; completed groups cannot receive duplicate jobs. Direct inventory dispatch is blocked when a linked delivery job must record proof.

Team workflow

  1. Verify the invoice and full payment.
  2. Allocate stock and review shortage purchase drafts.
  3. If the customer agrees to a partial delivery, split the shipment before creating jobs. Keep the immediately available quantities in the original shipment.
  4. Progress purchasing and receive goods in Inventory; run allocation again.
  5. Create each ready delivery job, assign driver/window, confirm the customer and progress the job with delivery proof.
  6. Complete required assembly with customer sign-off.
  7. Review actual costs on the order Payment tab. Use a service case for damage, returns or further work.

Validation

35 backend tests and 89 existing business-rule tests passed, including a disposable-database furniture journey covering concurrent allocation, shared incoming demand, reservation redistribution, unchanged invoices, stale split requests, job deduplication, partial dispatch and permission boundaries. Type checking and production build passed. Desktop/mobile browser checks use mocked business records; no synthetic orders were written to the working database.

Boundaries and remaining phases

  • Shipment splitting is for whole-unit furniture and happens before delivery/assembly jobs are created. Splitting an already scheduled or dispatched job, line cancellation and commercial repricing need a separate controlled revision workflow.
  • Goods must be represented in a physical inventory location and allocated before customer delivery. Direct supplier/carrier custody and proof handoff are not a substitute for warehouse stock allocation in this phase.
  • Purchasing, supplier tracking and carrier bookings require staff review. External supplier emails, transport APIs and automatic supplier invoice cost reconciliation are not connected.
  • Existing attachment/evidence facilities are reused. Offline driver capture, drawn signatures and route optimisation are not introduced here.
  • After-sales expansion, live Shopify credentials/sync rollout and production team onboarding remain separate phases. This is not a claim that the entire 69-page blueprint is finished.

Implementation

server/services/furniture.ts owns the connected workflow; components/furniture-fulfilment.tsx renders it. Routes: GET/POST /api/orders/:id/fulfilment. No schema migration or working-data seeding is required. Verification: tests/backend/foundation.test.ts and scripts/furniture-browser-check.ts.
